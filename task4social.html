DEFENSE STRATEGIES

Protecting against social engineering requires both knowledge and vigilance. Here are essential defense mechanisms:

Verification Protocols

Always verify requests for sensitive information or actions through a secondary channel.

Example: If someone calls asking for access, hang up and call them back using a known official number.

Password Hygiene

Use strong, unique passwords and never share them, even with apparent colleagues.

Pro Tip: Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords securely.

Physical Security

Be aware of tailgating attempts and never allow unauthorized individuals into secure areas.

Remember: It's okay to say no to someone requesting access without proper credentials.

Email Vigilance

Scrutinize all emails for red flags like urgency, mismatched sender addresses, or unusual requests.

Check: Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking.

Data Minimization

Share the minimum necessary information and only with verified parties.

Rule: If you're unsure whether to share information, don't - verify first.

Reporting Culture

Immediately report suspicious activity to your security team without fear of reprisal.

Important: Even if you're not sure, it's better to report and be wrong than stay silent.

The 3-Step Verification Process

1
Authenticate the Requestor

Verify the identity of the person making the request through known contact methods.

2
Validate the Request

Confirm the request is legitimate and necessary through independent channels.

3
Authorize Carefully

Only provide access or information after completing steps 1 and 2.
