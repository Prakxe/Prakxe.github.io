KNOWLEDGE TEST

This quiz will test your understanding of social engineering concepts covered in previous tasks. Select the best answer for each question. You'll receive immediate feedback after submitting your answers.

1. What is the primary goal of pretexting in social engineering?
Correct! Pretexting involves creating a fake scenario to manipulate victims into trusting the attacker.
Incorrect. The correct answer is: To create a fabricated scenario to establish trust.
2. Which of these is the BEST way to verify a suspicious request for sensitive information?
Correct! Always verify through a known, official communication channel different from how the request was received.
Incorrect. The best practice is to contact the requester through a known, official channel.
3. What psychological principle do attackers exploit when creating a sense of urgency?
Correct! Scarcity (limited time offers) creates pressure to act without careful consideration.
Incorrect. Attackers exploit scarcity by creating false urgency.
4. Which of these is a red flag for a phishing email?
Correct! Generic greetings are common in phishing attempts as attackers often don't know your name.
Incorrect. Generic greetings are a common red flag in phishing emails.
5. What should you do if you find an unfamiliar USB drive in your workplace?
Correct! Unknown USB devices could contain malware and should always be reported.
Incorrect. Never plug in unknown USB devices - report them to security immediately.
