HOW PHISHING TRICKS WORK

Scammers create fake versions of real websites and emails to steal your information. Here's how they do it in simple terms:

1

They Copy Real Websites

Scammers make fake copies of bank, email, or shopping sites that look almost identical to the real ones.

2

They Send Fake Messages

They send you a message with a fake link asking for your login or personal info.

3

They Steal Your Information

When you enter your info on their fake site, they capture it and use it to access your real accounts.

REAL LIFE EXAMPLE

Fake Amazon Order Alert: "Your order #54321 for $799.99 has been confirmed..."

How to Spot Fake Websites:

Feature Real Website Fake Website
Website Address paypal.com paypa1.com
Security Padlock 🔒 Shows company name 🔒 May show "Not Secure"
Design Quality Professional, no errors Slightly "off" looking
Urgent Messages Rarely demands immediate action "ACT NOW OR YOUR ACCOUNT WILL BE LOCKED!"
PROTECTION TIP

Always check the website address carefully before logging in.
