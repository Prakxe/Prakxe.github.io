TASK 5: DATA PROTECTION & BACKUP STRATEGIES

Protecting sensitive data and maintaining reliable backups are critical defenses against data loss, ransomware, and breaches.

⚠️

Data Disaster Reality Check

60% of companies that lose their data shut down within 6 months. Only 43% of businesses test their backups regularly, and 75% of those tests reveal failures.

The 3-2-1 Backup Rule

🔁
3 Copies of your data (original + 2 backups)
2 Different Media (external drive + cloud)
1 Offsite Copy (protected from local disasters)
Cloud Backup

Automated online backup services like Google Drive, Dropbox, or specialized backup solutions.

✓ Accessible anywhere
✓ Automatic versioning
✗ Ongoing costs
✗ Slower full restores
External Drives

Physical storage devices like HDDs, SSDs, or NAS systems kept in a secure location.

✓ Fast backup/restore
✓ One-time cost
✗ Can fail or be damaged
✗ Manual process
Hybrid Solution

Combination of cloud and local backups for maximum protection and flexibility.

✓ Best of both worlds
✓ Disaster recovery
✗ More complex
✗ Higher cost

Data Classification & Protection

Not all data requires the same level of protection. Classify data based on sensitivity:

Public
Company newsletters, marketing materials
Internal
Meeting notes, internal policies
Confidential
Employee records, contracts
Secret
Financial data, trade secrets

Backup Best Practices:

  • Test restores quarterly - backups are useless if they don't work
  • Automate backups to prevent human error
  • Versioning keeps multiple file versions (protects against ransomware)
  • Air-gap critical backups (disconnect after backup)
  • Document your backup procedures and recovery steps
