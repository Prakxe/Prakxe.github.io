TASK 2: CREATING STRONG PASSWORDS

A strong password is your first line of defense against cyber threats. Follow these principles to create passwords that are both secure and manageable:

Password Basics

Every strong password should have:

  • Minimum 12 characters
  • Uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Numbers and special characters
  • No personal information
Ex: J7#kL9!pQr2*

Passphrase Method

Combine random words for a memorable but strong password:

  • 4+ random words
  • Include special chars/numbers
  • Easy to remember
  • Hard to crack
Ex: CorrectHorseBatteryStaple!

Algorithm Method

Create a personal algorithm for site-specific passwords:

  • Base + site variation
  • Consistent but unique
  • Easy to regenerate
Ex: FB!J7#kL9!pQ (Facebook)

Test Your Password Strength

Enter a password to see its strength

Password Requirements:

  • At least 12 characters
  • Both uppercase and lowercase
  • At least one number
  • At least one special character

Common Password Mistakes:

  • Using personal information (names, birthdays, pet names)
  • Simple patterns (123456, qwerty, password)
  • Reusing passwords across multiple accounts
  • Writing passwords down in unsecured locations
  • Never changing passwords unless forced
