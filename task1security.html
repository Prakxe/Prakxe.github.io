TASK 1: PASSWORD SECURITY

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against cyber threats. A good password should be:

  • Long (at least 12 characters)
  • Complex (mix of upper/lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols)
  • Unique (not reused across accounts)
  • Unpredictable (avoid personal information or common phrases)

Password Strength Checker

Weak Fair Good Strong

Password Managers: Remembering complex passwords for every account is challenging. A password manager:

  • Generates and stores strong, unique passwords for all your accounts
  • Auto-fills passwords when you need them
  • Works across all your devices
  • Protects your passwords with one master password

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Even with a strong password, adding MFA provides an extra layer of security by requiring:

  • Something you know (password)
  • Something you have (phone, security key)
  • Something you are (fingerprint, face ID)
