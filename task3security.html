TASK 3: SAFE BROWSING & EMAIL HABITS

Most cyber attacks start with malicious websites or emails. Developing safe browsing and email habits is crucial for protecting yourself online.

⚠️

Did You Know?

94% of malware is delivered by email, and phishing websites account for 1 in 5 data breaches. Always verify before you click!

Secure Browser Practices:

HTTPS Everywhere
  • ✓ Always check for the padlock icon
  • ✓ Never enter sensitive data on HTTP sites
  • ✓ Look for "https://" not just "http://"
Security Extensions
  • ✓ Use ad-blockers (uBlock Origin)
  • ✓ Install privacy protectors (Privacy Badger)
  • ✓ Enable phishing protection
  • ✓ Keep extensions updated
Privacy Settings
  • ✓ Clear cookies regularly
  • ✓ Use private/incognito mode when needed
  • ✓ Disable third-party cookies
  • ✓ Review site permissions

Spotting Suspicious Emails:

Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended!
From: "Security Team" <no-reply@service-provider.com>

Dear User,

We've detected unusual activity on your account. To prevent suspension, you must verify your credentials immediately by clicking the link below:

https://fake-login.service-provider.com

If you don't take action within 24 hours, your account will be permanently disabled.

Sincerely,
Security Team

Red Flags Checklist:

✓
Generic greetings like "Dear User" instead of your name
✓
Urgent language creating panic ("Act now!")
✓
Suspicious sender addresses (check carefully)
✓
Links that don't match the official website URL
✓
Requests for sensitive information
✓
Poor spelling and grammar

Safe Download Practices:

  • Only download from official sources (developer websites, app stores)
  • Check file extensions - .exe files can be dangerous
  • Scan downloads with antivirus before opening
  • Be wary of "cracked" software or "free" premium content
  • Keep your software updated to patch security vulnerabilities
