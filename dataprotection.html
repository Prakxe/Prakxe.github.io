DATA PROTECTION

LEARN HOW TO PROTECT SENSITIVE INFORMATION FROM UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS, THEFT, OR LOSS. THIS MODULE COVERS BEST PRACTICES AND REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS.

1TASK 1: BRIEF

Introduction to data protection concepts and why securing sensitive information is critical for individuals and organizations.

2TASK 2: TYPES OF SENSITIVE DATA

Identify key categories of data that need protection including personal, financial, health, and intellectual property data.

3TASK 3: DATA THREATS

Understand common threats to data security including breaches, malware, insider threats, and social engineering.

4TASK 4: PROTECTION STRATEGIES

Learn best practices for data protection including encryption, access controls, backups, and employee training.

5TASK 5: COMPLIANCE & QUIZ

Explore key regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA) and test your knowledge with an assessment.
