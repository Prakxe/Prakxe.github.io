WHAT IS SOCIAL ENGINEERING?

The Human Firewall

Social engineering is the art of manipulating people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. Unlike technical hacking, it targets the weakest link in security: human behavior.

Why It Works

  • Trust exploitation: Attackers pretend to be someone you know or trust
  • Authority bias: People tend to comply with perceived authority figures
  • Urgency tactics: Creating false deadlines prevents careful thinking
  • Social proof: "Everyone else is doing it" mentality

Real-World Impact

60% of data breaches involve social engineering tactics (Verizon 2023 DBIR). The average cost of a social engineering attack is $4.5 million per incident.

Core Principles

  1. Pretexting: Creating a fake scenario to establish trust
  2. Phishing: Digital baiting via emails/messages
  3. Baiting: Offering something enticing to deliver malware
  4. Tailgating: Physical entry by following authorized personnel

Psychological Triggers

Attackers exploit these common human tendencies:

Reciprocity

"I did something for you, now you owe me." Attackers may offer fake help first.

Scarcity

"Limited time offer!" Creates fear of missing out.

Familiarity

Using names of real colleagues or mimicking internal communication styles.

Did You Know?

Even security professionals fall for social engineering 17% of the time in controlled tests. Regular training reduces this to under 3%.
