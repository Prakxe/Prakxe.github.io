TASK 2: PHISHING ATTACK TYPES

Phishing attacks come in many forms, each with unique characteristics. Understanding these variations helps you recognize and avoid them. Below are the most common types:

1. Email Phishing

The most common type. Attackers send fraudulent emails pretending to be from reputable companies to steal sensitive information.

2. Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks against specific individuals or organizations. Attackers use personal information to appear legitimate.

3. Smishing (SMS Phishing)

Phishing attempts via text messages. These often contain links to malicious websites or phone numbers.

4. Vishing (Voice Phishing)

Phone-based scams where attackers pretend to be from trusted organizations to extract sensitive information.

5. Clone Phishing

Attackers copy legitimate emails and replace attachments or links with malicious versions, then resend them.

6. Whaling

Targets high-profile employees like CEOs. These sophisticated attacks often involve legal or financial matters.

How to Recognize Phishing Attempts:

  • Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action
  • Requests for sensitive information (passwords, SSN, credit card)
  • Misspellings and poor grammar in professional communications
  • Suspicious sender email addresses that don't match the organization
  • Links that don't match the supposed sender's website
  • Unexpected attachments, especially from unknown senders
