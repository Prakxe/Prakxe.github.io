TASK 4: MITIGATIONS & PREVENTION

Now that you understand phishing threats, let's explore effective strategies to protect yourself and your organization. These mitigation techniques can significantly reduce your risk of falling victim to phishing attacks.

PROTECTION SUCCESS

Organizations that implement these techniques see up to 90% reduction in successful phishing attacks.

1. Email Verification

Always verify the authenticity of emails before taking any action.

  • Check sender's email address carefully
  • Look for spelling/grammar mistakes
  • Hover over links to see actual URLs
  • Verify unexpected attachments with sender

2. Multi-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security beyond passwords.

  • Enable MFA on all critical accounts
  • Use authenticator apps instead of SMS
  • Keep backup codes secure
  • Never approve unexpected MFA requests

3. Password Management

Strong, unique passwords for every account.

  • Use a password manager
  • Create long, complex passwords
  • Never reuse passwords across sites
  • Change passwords after potential breaches

4. Software Updates

Keep all systems patched and updated.

  • Enable automatic updates
  • Update operating systems regularly
  • Keep browsers and plugins current
  • Update mobile apps frequently

5. Security Awareness

Stay informed about current threats.

  • Participate in regular training
  • Follow cybersecurity news
  • Report suspicious emails immediately
  • Share knowledge with colleagues

6. Technical Controls

Implement security solutions for protection.

  • Use email filtering solutions
  • Enable browser phishing protection
  • Install endpoint security software
  • Configure firewalls properly

Phishing Response Checklist:

  • Don't panic - Stay calm and assess the situation
  • Don't click - Avoid links or attachments in suspicious messages
  • Verify - Contact the supposed sender through another channel
  • Report - Notify your IT/security team immediately
  • Change credentials - If you entered information, change passwords
  • Scan devices - Run antivirus scans if you opened attachments
  • Learn - Review what made the attempt convincing
REMEMBER

Security is everyone's responsibility. Your vigilance helps protect the entire organization.
