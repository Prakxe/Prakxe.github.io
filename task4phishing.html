HOW TO SPOT AND STOP SCAMS

Scammers try to trick you into giving away personal information. Here are easy ways to protect yourself:

GOOD NEWS!

People who use these tips avoid 90% of scams!

REAL LIFE EXAMPLE

The Fake Bank Alert: "Your account has been locked! Click here to unlock." The link goes to "your-bank-secure.com" (not your real bank's website). Always type your bank's address yourself instead of clicking links.

What To Do If You Think You're Being Scammed:

  • ✅ Stop - Don't click any links or download files
  • ✅ Verify - Contact the company using a phone number or website you know is real
  • ✅ Report - Tell your IT department or supervisor
  • ✅ Change - If you entered any passwords, change them immediately
  • ✅ Scan - Run a virus scan if you opened an attachment
YOU'RE NOT ALONE

Everyone makes mistakes. The important thing is to report suspicious messages so we can all stay safe together.
